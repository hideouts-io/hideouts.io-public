# Data notices and source rights

The Local Evidence Atlas is a mixed-source research collection. Its code license is not a license for every record displayed by the interface. This document is the canonical source-family notice; preserve these distinctions when copying, adapting or distributing the collection.

## Publication basis and scope

The owner has authorized the full prepared technical map for research publication and states that the work explains the system on personally owned consumer hardware. This records the owner's asserted research-publication basis. Ownership of a device does not by itself establish a vendor redistribution license. No independently verified Apple permission grant, Apple endorsement or blanket legal clearance is claimed.

The release retains real technical identifiers, coordinates, field values, coverage wire records, source comparisons and Flyover frames. Personal hardware identifiers, credentials, account/computer details and private access information are excluded from the prepared copies. Source-specific terms remain relevant; uncertainty about their applicability is recorded rather than presented as either an open-license grant or a determination that research publication is prohibited.

They are not redistribution licenses or evidence that this particular computer uploaded records, connected to the displayed cells or visited cataloged places.

## Keep evidence meanings separate

Returned position estimates, coverage-grid centers, virtual Flyover viewpoints, administrative pole/permit references and independently verified installations answer different questions. The target full NR identity still has no supported measured PCI assignment and no independently verified physical-site link. Preserve uncertainty, null fields, source dates and raw unknown/sentinel values. Data availability and numerical precision do not establish accuracy, completeness, a user's movements or present infrastructure operation.
