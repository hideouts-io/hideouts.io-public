What is included. Every retained row from all 12 Atlas layers: 38,511 LTE estimates, 100 NR estimates, 60,168 coverage grids, 6 raw outliers, 334 Flyover tours, 1,432 catalog anchors, 400 Wi-Fi estimates, 915 City small-cell points, 405 permit records, two independently sourced cell estimates, and one unplotted engineering-plan record. All 5,286 tour camera frames and complete coverage wire data remain accessible.

What the sources establish. Full identities can associate returned estimates. Coverage PCI references can establish network/channel context. A public pole, a shared PCI, a precise-looking coordinate, or nearby records do not establish an identity-to-antenna link.

What time means. Database first/last dates, permit dates, retrieval timestamps and actual observations are different fields. No mapped row establishes a personal movement timeline. Original NAD83 planned coordinates remain documentary and unplotted.
