How we build

Security tools should be the most trustworthy software on your machine.

  • Observe first, change explicitly

    Scanners and inspectors are read-only. Tools that do change something, like mounting a volume or disabling an interface, say so and ask first.

  • Local-first, no telemetry

    Tools run on your machine with no accounts or analytics. Where one needs the network, its README says exactly why.

  • Observation vs. interpretation

    Every tool and write-up separates what was directly seen from what it might mean.

  • Evidence you can verify

    Hashed outputs, sanitized screenshots, and reproducible steps, so you don’t have to take our word for it.
